Privacy Policy

App: Global YDS (VocabMaster) · Developer: Cynorra · Package: com.cynorra.vocabmaster.app
Last updated: October 17, 2026

Account & Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@cynorra.com from your registered address with the subject Account Deletion Request - Global YDS. Your data is permanently deleted within 7 days. Full details.

1. Data we process

  • Account: email, username and password (stored hashed by Firebase Authentication). With Google Sign-In, your Google account email and name.
  • Progress: learned words, exam results, mistakes, scores and statistics, stored in Firebase Firestore linked to your account and locally on your device (Room/SQLite).
  • Leaderboard: your username and total score are visible to other users. Your email address is not shown.
  • Translation: when you look up a word, that word is sent to the MyMemory translation service (api.mymemory.translated.net). No account data is sent.
  • Analytics & crashes: Firebase Analytics (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports, device model, OS version).
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may use the device advertising ID (AAID) and similar technical data to serve ads. Users in the EEA/UK see Google's UMP consent form and can change their choice at any time. You can reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Notifications: daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no data is sent to a server. You can disable them in Android settings.

2. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed only by the services that run the app: Google Play Services, Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config), Google AdMob and MyMemory (translation), each governed by its own privacy policy. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

3. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account and is removed within 7 days of a deletion request. Analytics and crash data follow Google's standard retention periods.

4. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You may access, correct, delete, object to the processing of, and port your data. Contact support@cynorra.com.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we have, we delete the account.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page with its date.
