Delete Your Account & Data

Global YDS (VocabMaster) · Developer: Cynorra · com.cynorra.vocabmaster.app

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@cynorra.com from the address you registered with.
  2. Use the subject Account Deletion Request - Global YDS.
  3. State the account email you want deleted.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account: email, username and credentials.
  • Your Firestore records: learned words, exam results, mistakes, scores, statistics and your leaderboard entry.

Timeline & retention

Your data is permanently deleted within 7 days of your request. Nothing is retained afterwards except where required by law. Anonymous analytics and crash data that cannot identify you may remain under Google's standard retention periods.

Data stored on your device

Local progress is removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Global YDS > Storage > Clear data.
